# Welcome to ModusBrain

**ModusBrain** is an operational knowledge engine for AI agents. While most company knowledge bases stop at simple search and chat, ModusBrain compiles policies, wiki runbooks, and tribal knowledge into **approved operational skills** with full audit logs and safety gates.

With ModusBrain, autonomous agents act consistently, not creatively.

---

## Key Capabilities

ModusBrain adds the final layer of safety to agent execution:

* **Compile** — Turn prose guides, runbooks, and policies into versioned, standing operational skills via the command line:
  `modusbrain opskill compile "topic"`
* **Gate** — Establish risk tiers, minimum confidence thresholds, and manual human approval token overrides for high-stakes actions.
* **Prove** — Maintain a cryptographic action audit log tracking executing agent IDs, evaluated context, and approval records.
