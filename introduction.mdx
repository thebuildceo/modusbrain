# Introduction to ModusBrain

**ModusBrain** is an advanced operational knowledge engine specifically engineered to solve the safety and consistency challenges faced by autonomous AI agents. While traditional corporate knowledge bases and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) platforms are designed primarily for conversational search and basic Q&A, ModusBrain provides an execution-oriented layer. It systematically compiles scattered policy documents, internal wiki runbooks, and team guidelines into highly structured, versioned, and **approved operational skills** protected by automated safety gates and comprehensive cryptographic audit trails.

By deploying ModusBrain as your agentic runtime boundary, you ensure that autonomous systems act consistently and follow exact company protocols, eliminating the risk of creative hallucinations or policy violations during high-stakes tasks.

---

## Key Capabilities

ModusBrain bridges the gap between static retrieval and safe agent execution by establishing a secure operational lifecycle:

* **High-Fidelity Compilation** — ModusBrain automatically reads and parses your raw markdown documentation, wikis, and policies, translating unstructured prose into versioned, static operational skill runbooks with executable logic via a simple command-line interface:
  `modusbrain opskill compile "topic"`
* **Adaptive Confidence Gating** — Every action requested by an agent is evaluated in real time against the active policy context. ModusBrain calculates a confidence score and applies strict gating rules based on configurable risk tiers, allowing operators to require manual human approval tokens for sensitive or low-confidence operations.
* **Cryptographic Action Auditing** — Every skill execution, decision parameter, confidence calculation, and operator approval is written to a secure, queryable audit ledger. This ensures total operational transparency, simplifies compliance reviews, and provides detailed logs for debugging agent behaviors.
