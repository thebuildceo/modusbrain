# Welcome to ModusBrain

**ModusBrain** turns scattered company knowledge into **versioned, confidence-gated operational skills** that AI agents execute against — not just search.

Most "company brain" tools stop at chat and retrieval. ModusBrain adds the last mile:

- **Compile** — Draft skills from wikis, policies, and runbooks (`modusbrain opskill compile`)
- **Gate** — Risk tiers, confidence scores, human approval before high-stakes actions
- **Prove** — Full action audit trail for compliance and debugging

## Built on ModusBrain

ModusBrain extends the open-source [ModusBrain](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain) engine (MIT License). ModusBrain provides hybrid RAG search, synthesis, graph traversal, and freshness. ModusBrain adds the safe-execution layer for autonomous company operations.
