**ModusBrain** is built for teams and organizations that want to move beyond individual AI interactions and deploy autonomous agents that understand and follow company-wide policies — consistently, at scale.

Whether you are a small startup onboarding your first AI agent or a large enterprise running dozens of agents across different departments, ModusBrain gives your entire organization a **single, authoritative source of operational truth** that every agent executes against.

---

## The Problem ModusBrain Solves for Teams

When multiple people and agents work from the same company knowledge, three problems emerge immediately:

- **Inconsistency** — Different agents give different answers to the same question because they each interpret raw documents differently.
- **Risk** — Agents take high-stakes actions (processing refunds, sending emails, modifying records) without any safety gate between them and your company policies.
- **No Accountability** — When something goes wrong, there is no audit trail to understand what an agent did, what version of a policy it used, or who approved the action.

ModusBrain solves all three. It compiles your company's tribal knowledge, runbooks, and policies into versioned, **approved operational skills** — and enforces them consistently across every agent that connects to your brain.

## How a Company Brain Is Set Up

Setting up ModusBrain for a full team takes about 30 minutes. The setup has three main steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run the server">
    Deploy the ModusBrain HTTP MCP server on your infrastructure. It ships with a built-in OAuth 2.1 authorization layer, so each team member gets their own scoped credentials — with `read`, `write`, and `admin` access tiers.

    ```bash
    modusbrain serve --http --port 3000
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your team's AI clients">
    Each team member installs the ModusBrain thin-client on their machine and connects their coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or any MCP-aware editor) to the shared brain server using their personal token.

    ```bash
    modusbrain init --mcp-only --mcp-url https://brain.your-company.com/mcp
    claude mcp add modusbrain -- modusbrain serve
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Compile and approve your first company skill">
    Your policy owners compile your first operational skill from your existing internal documentation. Once reviewed and approved, every connected agent immediately has access to the live, policy-gated version.

    ```bash
    modusbrain opskill compile "expense reimbursement" --risk-tier high_stakes --owner finance@company.com
    modusbrain opskill approve expense-reimbursement --by finance@company.com
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Team Roles & Permissions

ModusBrain is designed around a clear permission model that separates the people who set policy from the agents who execute it:

| Role | What They Do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Policy Owner** | Compiles skills, approves drafts, issues approval tokens, submits corrections |
| **Team Member** | Connects their agent to the brain, queries the knowledge graph, triggers skill execution |
| **Admin** | Manages user credentials, access scopes, and server configuration |
| **Agent** | Executes approved skills, requests confidence evaluations, logs audit records |

## Multi-Database and Scale

For teams with large knowledge bases (1,000+ documents) or multi-machine setups, ModusBrain supports a full **PostgreSQL + pgvector** backend via Supabase or any hosted Postgres provider. You can start with the zero-configuration embedded PGLite database and migrate later with a single command:

```bash
modusbrain migrate --to supabase
```
