ModusBrain gives a team one place for the knowledge behind its work. Your people and AI agents can use the same current context instead of relying on disconnected documents or private chat history.

It is useful when you need agents to be helpful **and** consistent: they should understand the right source material, follow clear guidance, and leave a trace for important work.

## A simple team workflow

1. **Choose the sources.** Keep shared docs, team-specific material, and sensitive information in the right source.
2. **Connect the people and agents.** Use the MCP server to make the brain available to the tools your team already uses.
3. **Set the operating guidance.** Turn stable policies and runbooks into reviewed operational skills when a repeatable workflow needs more control.
4. **Keep it current.** Sync source changes, maintain the brain, and review the history when decisions need to be explained.
